Key Features

🌱

Smart Plant Management

Low-maintenance plant care for busy makers. Soil moisture sensor enables automatic watering management.

💡

RGB LED Control

Beautiful lighting effects with 5V 3-pin RGB LEDs and NeoPixel. Perfectly recreates gaming PC atmosphere.

📊

Environmental Monitoring

Monitor indoor environment with CO2 sensor module. Maintain optimal conditions for both plants and humans.

🤖

IoT Integration

Comprehensive IoT system using M5Stack, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi. Real-time data visualization.

⚡

Automatic Control

Auto/manual mode switchable relay control. Flexible operation according to your needs.

🎮

Gaming Design

Stylish design integrated into PC case. Perfect balance of functionality and aesthetics.
